Anything smaller is ignored (carpet bits). Slide it and a square shows the size on the picture: make it a bit smaller than your smallest Lego.
One Lego shown as several pieces? Slide toward more. Two touching Legos shown as one? Slide toward less.

How it tells pieces apart: it compares with the empty floor it saw first, so it can see even carpet-coloured bricks and ignores shadows. Different colours split by colour. Two same-colour bricks touching are split at the thin dark seam between them, or at the corner where they meet. Two bricks snapped together are one piece (the arm moves them together).
